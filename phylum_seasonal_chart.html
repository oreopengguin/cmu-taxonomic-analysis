Phylum-level community composition shifts by season

Relative abundance of each phylum among the 20 most-abundant OTUs, computed from season-specific read counts in top20_otu_table.tsv. Each bar sums the season's top-20 reads to 100%. Phyla with mixed-confidence evidence are split into a solid (high-identity) and hatched (low-identity) sub-segment.

Hatched, * segments are reads from OTUs whose closest BLAST reference match was below ~90% percent identity, so the phylum assignment for that share of reads is less certain than a high-identity (solid-fill) match. Unresolved covers OTUs whose top hits were non-bacterial (plant sequences) or uninformative ("uncultured bacterium"), so no phylum could be assigned — see bacteria-list.csv for per-OTU detail.
